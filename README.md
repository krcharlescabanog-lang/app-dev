# **PROJECT HAIL MARY**
## *About the Movie*
Project Hail Mary is a science fiction story based on the one of the best novel of the genre by *Andy Weir*. Where Ryland Grace wakes up alone in a spaceship with amnesia.

## Basic Information
| Information | Details |
| ----------- | ------- |
| Title | Project Hail Mary |
| Genre | Science Fiction, Adventure |
| Author | Andy Weir |
| Main Character | Ryland Grace |
| Setting | Outer Space |

## Plot
Grace goes on a space mission to save Earth from an unknown problem regarding the Sun while recovering his memories from coma-induced amnesia. During this mission, he encounters a friendly lonely alien that went with the same mission, named Rocky and became able to communicate though deciphering the language of their species, translate and vocalize through an Ai-voice. The two of them combined their smarts and completed both of their mission.

## Review
I like Project Hail Mary because it has an interesting story about space, science, interspecies friendship, hope and rediscovery. The duo enamored me with their silly quirks and capabilities, and solving problem by combining them.

## Ratings
**10 / 10** It has everything that I liked and loved without introducing a negative reaction from me.
